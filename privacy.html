🐾 Purrfeed Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Purrfeed is an app for watching kitten videos. It is published by Enemies by Nature LLC.

What we collect

Nothing. Purrfeed has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no tracking. We do not collect, store or sell any personal information.

Data on your device

Your saved (favorite) videos and your daily streak are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

Third-party content and links

Videos are streamed from Pexels and credited to their creators. Product links open Amazon.com; as an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases. When you open Amazon or Pexels, their own privacy policies apply.

Children

Purrfeed is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.
